# Sales kit — from cold list to three signed LOIs by day 45

The 90-day plan's binding constraint is not engineering, it is **three signed design partners by day 45**. This folder is everything that touches that constraint outside the code.

| File | What it is | When it is used |
|---|---|---|
| `../08-sales-playbook.md` | Call structure, discovery questions, 12 objections with answers, disqualification rules, follow-up cadence | Open before every call |
| `../prospect/` | Feasibility screener + one-page opportunity review generator | Before the call (who to call) and after (the leave-behind) |
| `../06-design-partner-loi.docx` | The document that gets signed | When they say "how do we start" |
| `../04-design-partner-measurement-plan.docx` | What you will be judged on, agreed in writing | At signature |
| `outreach-templates.md` | The messages themselves | Daily |
| `first-45-days-activity.md` | Week-by-week numbers to hit | Weekly review |

## Why a sales kit belongs in an engineering pack

Because the pack's own verdict was that the plan fails on distribution, not on product. A solo operator who writes excellent measurement code and makes four calls a week will fail with a perfectly measured failure. The screener, the playbook and the templates exist so that the *sales* side gets the same treatment the engineering side got: written down, with numbers attached, and a way to tell whether it is working.

## The one metric

**Signed LOIs that pay, by day 45: three.** Not calls made, not conversations had, not proposals sent. Two signed is a partial success worth continuing at a reduced scope; one or zero by day 45 is the kill criterion firing, and the honest response is to stop and reconsider rather than extend the deadline.

## The four numbers to track weekly

| Metric | Target by day 45 | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Hotels screened out as NOT A FIT | ≥ 15 of ~40 | If nothing is being disqualified, the screen isn't being applied |
| First calls held | ≥ 20 | The pipeline volume the plan needs |
| Data shares received (rung 3) | ≥ 8 | The rung that converts most |
| LOIs signed | **3** | The only number that counts |

Full week-by-week schedule in `first-45-days-activity.md`.
